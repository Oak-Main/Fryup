# Cookie Policy

## Application storage
- `fryup_favourites` stores saved recipe choices.
- `fryup_cookie_consent` records the banner choice, including Essential Only.
- You can clear these values in your browser settings.

## Optional measurement
The custom-domain proxy's automatic Google Analytics insertion is paused. Accept All does not activate that paused tag. Platform-managed cookies and telemetry require a separate inventory. The observed `cf_zaraz_client` cookie has not been assigned a verified purpose or retention period in this review.

## Questions
Contact [privacy@fryup.uk](mailto:privacy@fryup.uk) or use [Contact & Corrections](/contact/). The complete notice and consent design require qualified review before optional measurement is enabled.
